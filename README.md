## Right now

I'm working on **[CalcQuest](https://calcquest.app)**. A brain puzzle that somehow caught fire and grew to over 25,000 users across 150+ countries. I also raised a [1 lakh grant](https://calcquest.app) from my college incubation center to back it.

[![App Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/App_Store-0D96F6?style=for-the-badge&logo=apple&logoColor=white)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calc-quest-a-calculator-game/id6757161847)
[![Play Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/Play_Store-34A853?style=for-the-badge&logo=googleplay&logoColor=white)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.calcquest.calculatorgame)
![25k+ users](https://img.shields.io/badge/25k%2B-users-D93A2B?style=for-the-badge)
![150+ countries](https://img.shields.io/badge/150%2B-countries-1f2937?style=for-the-badge)

I get crazy dopamine when I randomly see [somebody](https://x.com/MSiekmoeller/status/1981704468631810390) using something I made.

Alongside that, I'm a Flutter engineer at [MedGen Japan](https://medgen.nihinmedia.jp), building an AI medical assistant app for doctors in Japan.
